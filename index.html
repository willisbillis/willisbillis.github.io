About

Astrophysics-trained analyst turned immunology-focused computational biologist, building practical tooling for translational science.

I currently support collaborative work between the Boss-Scharer and Kohlmeier labs, with an emphasis on single-cell and multimodal profiling of adaptive immune responses. My focus is delivery: robust pipelines, high-quality statistical interpretation, and communication that helps wet-lab and clinical teams act confidently.

Before bioinformatics, I studied astrophysical fluid dynamics and observational astronomy. That training now translates into strong numerical intuition, systems-level thinking, and comfort with complex, high-dimensional data.
